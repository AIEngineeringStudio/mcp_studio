JOB DESCRIPTION

Joining Amex Tech means discovering and shaping your contribution to something big. Here, you can work alongside talented tech teams and build a unique career with the Powerful Backing of American Express. With a range of opportunities to work with the latest technologies, and a commitment to back the broader engineering community through open source, our mission is to power your success. Because Amex Tech is powered by our technology, our culture, and our colleagues.
The Technology organization enables and accelerates the company’s growth strategies, delivering global capabilities and services in support of Amex’s customers and colleagues, while maintaining 24/7 servicing and availability to ensure an uninterrupted, high-quality customer experience. Technology provides the foundation for everything we do in the company while driving differentiation through building and leveraging innovative technology and data insights.
Global Commercial Services (GCS) serves millions of business customers around the world, from mom-and-pop shops to approximately 70% of the S&P 500. We are the number one issuer of small business cards, the industry leader in corporate T&E and represent approximately 40% of the company’s total revenues. Our vision is to be essential to our customers’ businesses every day. We do that by offering a diverse suite of payment and cashflow tools our customers need to run and grow their businesses, from a wide range of traditional card products, to working capital and supply chain financing, to new digital solutions that make it easy for our customers to manage their financial and payment needs.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Sr Solutions Architect I leads alignment of technology platforms with company roadmaps, optimizes integration and performance, oversees solution architecture and application programming interface strategy, mentors engineering teams, and collaborates on technical strategy and improvements. Mentors junior Solutions Architects and cross-functional team of colleagues, fostering a culture of excellence and innovation
Identifies and implements technical solutions and business process improvements by analyzing business needs and leveraging innovative technologies
Collaborates with business partners and product engineering teams to enforce architecture practices, ensuring alignment with architectural and technical strategy through governance and project reviews
Leads technology platform solutions alignment with platform blueprints and company roadmaps, serving as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) and coordinating with relevant stakeholders
Optimizes cross-platform integration implementing advanced integration strategies to enhance platform capabilities, performance, and efficiency
Ensures the engineered environment meets comprehensive business, application design, and infrastructure requirements by overseeing platform integration performance
Executes solution architecture design strategies, leads design and code reviews to ensure the delivery of desired business outcomes and adherence to enterprise standards
Supervises the platform's application programming interface strategy and implementation roadmap by collaborating with business and data architects, incorporating their input into cohesive platform solutions
Collaborates and co-creates effectively with teams in product and the business to align technology initiatives with business objectives
Embeds AI-assisted architecture tools into team workflows, improving design analysis, documentation, and platform integration outcomes
Mentors junior architects on responsible use of AI-enabled tools for solution design, API strategy, and requirement analysis
Applies AI-assisted capabilities to improve architecture reviews, business process analysis, and technical solution recommendations across the platform
QUALIFICATIONS

Hands-on programming experience relevant to the assigned technology platform in a professional environment, and/or comparable experience
Experience in application solution architecture design and end-to-end software development of complex distributed (multi-tiered) systems
Experience in multiple technology disciplines with a deep knowledge of architectural concepts (business, data, technical, and solution) and a proven track record of implementation
Experience in using a systems analysis and design methodology applicable to an Agile product environment
Experience in relevant software development life cycle methodologies, practices, and compliance policies/procedures
Experience in solution architecture, complex application systems design, and platform integration via modern approaches (i.e., RESTful application programming interfaces)
Experience leading adoption of AI-assisted tools within solution architecture or platform integration teams
Experience using AI-enabled capabilities to improve architecture analysis, design quality, and documentation practices
Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, and/or comparable experience; advanced degree preferred
Have passed (or pass within 6 months) a Senior Engineer level DT Technical Assessment in at least one technology discipline relevant to the assigned Technology Platform(s)
Knowledge of relevant supporting architecture domains required to align and deliver solutions (business, information, application, technical, etc.)
Knowledge of Service Oriented Architecture design principles, execution patterns, and performance optimization techniques
Knowledge of current industry best practices regarding solution delivery disciplines
Knowledge of AI-enabled architecture tools and their application within solution delivery, integration, and documentation workflows
Understanding of governance considerations for AI-assisted architecture work, including validation of outputs and control of design risk
